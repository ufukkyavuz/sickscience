I put a blonde guest in the closing shot as "Merve" (photographer IMG_0099, NetWork launch) because she was blonde and looked similar; the user caught it ("sarışın diye benzettin galiba").

**Why:** a wrong name on a real person in a client video is embarrassing and can't be shipped; founders (Merve = blonde, Polen = dark hair) appear among many guests with the same hair.

**How to apply:** only use shots where the founder is verified by context (on the mic at their own launch, the known Welcome-wall pair take, their own challenge videos) — not by hair or a quick face comparison. If a new photo is wanted and identity isn't certain, show it to the user and ask before using it.
