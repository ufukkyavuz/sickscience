Friday-13 frames (2026-11-16): Story layout had price lines low, but Feed/Square crops put them on top of the bottles/ladder, the receipt headline overflowed the width (fs120 → 84), and a seal overlapped the price. Fixed by moving price lines up under the headline and the seal to a clear zone.

**Why:** Feed/Square are crops of the same 9:16 master ([[feedback-same-image-all-formats]]), so products sit lower in the frame than in Story.

**How to apply:** after building Story, screenshot Feed + Square and check each text/seal against the product bbox; price goes right under the headline in the top band (left-align if the tube top is centred); headline fit = measured width ≤ ~960 px; seal never beside the price line. Square often drops the offer line, so keep headline + price + seal only ([[feedback-no-tiny-text-be-smart]], [[feedback-check-layout-full-size]]).
