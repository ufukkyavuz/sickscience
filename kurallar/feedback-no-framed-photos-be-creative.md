User (2026-10-01, SickScience) hates framed/rectangular-cropped photos in designs ("çerçeve içine alınmış kesilmiş fotoğraflardan nefret ederim"). A photo is either a full-page background or a transparent PNG cutout — nothing in between. Plain side-by-side packshot composites were rejected as "dümdüz".

**Why:** Boxed crops look amateur and break the premium look; flat lineups say nothing.
**How to apply:** Every visual must be a creative ad concept: tell something about the product (benefit, ingredient, clinical result) and talk offer/discount when one exists (bundle savings, free shipping threshold, GWP). Use the client's own cutout PNGs (SickScience: Products/png/) rather than cutting products out of web photos myself.

**Videos too (2026-10-06):** a 1:1 TV clip placed as a block on a 9:16 canvas with colour bands was rejected ("böyle kesik çok hoşuma gitmedi"). Ad video is full-bleed per format (9:16 = 608×1080 source crop upscaled, captions overlaid in safe zone). Before/after stills from footage must be rebuilt cleanly (crop each panel, remove baked labels, show jaw/neck fully) — a misaligned letterboxed B/A was called "kaymış".

2026-10-20 (Amazon What's inside): user again — "defalarca kez söyledim kesik görsel sevmiyorum". Applies to web/Amazon modules too: no photo tiles/cards with hard edges; use cutout PNGs (macOS Vision subject lift) on a shared baseline or a full-bleed scene. Fewer labels = more premium.
