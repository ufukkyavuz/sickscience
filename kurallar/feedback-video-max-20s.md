Every video ad (UGC, flow edits, press clips) must be 20 s or shorter, end card included. The 42 s and 26–30 s versions of the ShapeShift flow were both rejected as too long, even though Buzzfy's skincare data shows a 45–75 s "best band".

**Why:** User 2026-10-09: "videolar yine çok uzun ya maks 20 olacak şekilde ayarlayalım".

**How to apply:**
- Plan the beat list against a 20 s budget before generating clips: hook ≤3 s, one problem beat, one product beat, a short offer, a 1.5 s end card.
- Drop whole beats rather than racing the speech (speed ≤1.1×).
