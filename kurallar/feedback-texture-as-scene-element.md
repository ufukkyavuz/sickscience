User, 2026-10-21, angry about the batch-72 texture videos (hands stretching and rubbing gel): "Slime mi oynuyorsun? … Bahsettiğim şey görselde arkada, önünde, sağında, solunda bir yerde ürünün dokusunun olması … hazır çekimlerde varsa onlar da olur … sıfırdan video yap demedim."

**Why:** buyers convert more when they see the texture. But the texture is a supporting element of a normal product visual, not the subject of a new video. Hands playing with gel read as slime and are nonsensical for a premium serum.

**How to apply:**
- In product shots and ads, add the real gel-serum texture as a scene element next to the bottle:
  - a swatch or smear on the surface
  - a glossy dollop behind it
  - a drop on the pump or dropper
  - a swipe on glass in the foreground
- Keep the texture translucent and milky, per [[feedback-product-texture-gel-serum]].
- Prefer existing footage or stills that already show texture: client Brand Assets, Dropbox UGC, earlier generations.
- Don't build stand-alone "hand plays with gel" films.
- Never stretch, pull or rub gel between fingers.
