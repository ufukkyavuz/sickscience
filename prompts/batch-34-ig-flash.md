Source: "SickScience Social Media Plan" sheet, tab "October Social". The posts are 12, 15, 18 and 29 October; captions come from the sheet.

Goal: the current posts look AI-made (smooth studio light, plastic perfection). Direct on-camera flash adds the editorial, real-camera texture they are missing: hard shadow, specular hot spots on chrome, slight vignette and real skin.

Common LIGHT AND SHADOW block, adapted per scene:
- One hard direct flash mounted on the camera, slightly above the lens, firing straight at the subject.
- Crisp dark flash shadow cast on the wall/surface just behind and below the subject.
- Bright specular flash hot spots on chrome; quick light fall-off into a darker background; subtle natural vignette.
- Never a soft studio softbox look.
- Flash glare never covers any printed word; all text on the products stays legible.
