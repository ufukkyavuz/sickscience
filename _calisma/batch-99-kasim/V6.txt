A real bright science campaign photograph. The idea: the three formulas of the Vault set held in a polished steel laboratory clamp stand on a white lab bench, clean and precise.

Image 1 is a real photo of the ShapeShift capsule and shows the true construction of every capsule in this brand: a slim rounded pill, its glossy coloured chrome cap is exactly the upper half, the lower half is mirror-polished silver chrome, rounded at both ends. The brand words are printed in thin pure white letters running vertically, directly on the chrome of the cap and of the silver half. The word SickScience is printed only on the coloured cap, and the product name (ShapeShift, NetWork or PowerCycle) only on the silver half, each once. There is NO printed band, strip or label behind the letters. PROPORTIONS ARE CRITICAL: the ShapeShift and NetWork capsules are slim and tall, height 3.4 times width, never stubby. Image 2 shows the PowerCycle capsule: glossy GREEN chrome cap, the largest capsule. Image 3 shows the DropOff tube: matte cobalt blue squeeze tube on its blue cap. Take every capsule's construction from image 1. Exactly three products: green PowerCycle capsule, cobalt DropOff tube, violet ShapeShift capsule. No extra product.

PRODUCT ORIENTATION

The DropOff tube stands upright in the centre, held gently at its middle by a steel clamp of a lab retort stand. The PowerCycle capsule stands on the bench to the left of the stand base, the ShapeShift capsule to the right. Labels face the camera, text vertical. Camera level with the bench, straight on.

COMPOSITION AND FRAMING

Vertical 9:16 frame, 70mm lens. The retort stand rod rises on the left edge. The group is horizontally centred inside the middle 60% of the frame width, from 48% to 80% of the frame height. A white lab bench fills the bottom 20%. The band from 10% to 40% of the frame height is clean bright white-grey space for a headline. A clear beaker and a small petri dish with a translucent gel-serum bead stand softly out of focus behind.

SCALE

If the ShapeShift capsule is 100 tall, PowerCycle is 117 tall and 25% wider, the DropOff tube is 165 tall. The beaker is 15 cm tall.

LIGHT AND SHADOW

Bright soft daylight from a large window on the left, crisp highlights on the chrome and the steel stand, soft grey contact shadows. Every printed word lit and readable.

BACKGROUND

Bright white-grey lab wall, soft and plain.

PHOTOGRAPHIC FINISH

f/5.6. Polished steel, mirror chrome, matte tube, thin clear glass, a translucent gel-serum bead (semi-transparent with a soft milky haze, neither opaque cream nor water-clear). Cool clean white grade, high-key. Fine photographic grain. Ultra photorealistic, hyperrealistic, indistinguishable from a real photograph, 8K detail. All product text must be perfectly legible, sharp, and accurate, no blurry or distorted letters. No text, no letters, no logos added to the scene; only the products' own printed words.

INTEGRATION

The products are physically in this scene: same light direction, softness and colour temperature, their chrome reflects this environment, real contact shadow, real reflection on the surface below, same sharpness and grain as the scene, no outline, no halo.
