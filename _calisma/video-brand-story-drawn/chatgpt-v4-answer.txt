2. SickScience — English narrator script

Third-person narrator · Approx. 115–120 seconds · 22 narrative segments

Writing

For the first time, here's how SickScience began. With two geneticists.

Polen Koçak-Denizci and Merve Yıldırım-Canpolat met in a chemistry and biology class in 2009.

They worked together for fourteen years. Both earned biotechnology PhDs at Yeditepe University. Polen was also a research scholar at Harvard–MIT Health Sciences and Technology.

Together, they studied stem cell production, regenerative medicine, and exosome applications in cancer research. Their work includes international patents and twenty to thirty peer-reviewed papers.

For Merve, the research was personal. Her mother had mesothelioma, with no treatment available. Their research was still preclinical when her mother passed away.

Afterward, they finished and published the research. Then they made a decision: take science beyond the lab, into people's homes.

At the center of their work were exosomes. Tiny carriers, roughly one-thousandth the size of a cell. Like delivery drones, they carry coded messages between cells.

Their exosomes come from plants: pineapple, garlic, wheat, and radish. A library of thousands of plant messages. One hundred percent plant-based and vegan.

Then came NX35. A biomimetic platform combining exosome separation, room-temperature stabilization without a cold chain, and their plant library.

Why beauty? Everyone says they're a scientist. Most are marketers.

These two wanted clinical-level science in formulas people could use at home.

Then La Mer's marketing director saw their data and said, “That's sick!”

Their answer? “No, that's science.” And that's how SickScience got its name.

ShapeShift for the neck and jawline. NetWork for the face. PowerCycle for hair. DropOff for the body.

Four products. Fourteen awards, including recognition from Oprah, InStyle, and Esquire.

Science, but never boring.

Bu metinde annesinin hikâyesi bir ürün iddiasına değil, kurucuların bilimi laboratuvar dışına taşıma motivasyonuna bağlanıyor. Exosome araştırmasının geçmişi ile kozmetik ürünlerin işlevleri birbirinden ayrı tutuluyor.
