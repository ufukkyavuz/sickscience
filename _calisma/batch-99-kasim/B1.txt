A real science campaign photograph of two products. The idea: the body duo standing on a sheet of wet black glass, lit by one cold strip of light.

Image 1 is a real photo of the ShapeShift capsule and shows the true construction of every capsule in this brand: a slim rounded pill, its glossy coloured chrome cap is exactly the upper half, the lower half is mirror-polished silver chrome, rounded at both ends. The brand words are printed in thin pure white letters running vertically, directly on the chrome of the cap and of the silver half. The word SickScience is printed only on the coloured cap, and the product name (ShapeShift, NetWork or PowerCycle) only on the silver half, each once. There is NO printed band, strip or label behind the letters. PROPORTIONS ARE CRITICAL: the ShapeShift and NetWork capsules are slim and tall, height 3.4 times width, never stubby. Image 2 shows the DropOff tube: a matte cobalt blue squeeze tube standing upright on its blue cap, white vertical lettering; copy its design exactly. Exactly two products: the DropOff tube and the violet ShapeShift capsule. No extra product.

PRODUCT ORIENTATION

The tube stands upright on its cap, slightly behind and left. The violet capsule stands upright in front of it to the right, overlapping the tube a little. Labels face the camera, text vertical. Camera low, level with the middle of the capsule.

COMPOSITION AND FRAMING

Vertical 9:16 frame, 85mm lens. Both products horizontally centred inside the middle 50% of the frame width, standing from 46% to 78% of the frame height. Behind them, softly out of focus, one tall clear graduated glass cylinder on the left and a round-bottom flask on the right, cropped by the frame edges. The band from 10% to 40% of the frame height is calm, dark and empty for a headline. The bottom 20% is the black glass surface with reflections.

SCALE

If the ShapeShift capsule is 100 tall, the DropOff tube is 165 tall. The capsule is 12 cm tall and 3.5 cm wide, the tube 20 cm tall. The graduated cylinder is 40 cm tall.

LIGHT AND SHADOW

One long horizontal white strip light from above and behind draws bright clean vertical highlights down the chrome and the tube edges. Soft frontal fill keeps every printed word readable. Thin bright rim lines on the glass.

BACKGROUND

Deep slate grey-blue falling to near black at the top. Wet black glass surface with fine water droplets and a mirror reflection of the products.

PHOTOGRAPHIC FINISH

f/5.6. Monochrome steel-blue grade: everything is silver, slate and blue-grey, the only saturated colours are the violet capsule and the cobalt tube. Fine photographic grain. Ultra photorealistic, hyperrealistic, indistinguishable from a real photograph, 8K detail. All product text must be perfectly legible, sharp, and accurate, no blurry or distorted letters. No text, no letters, no logos added to the scene; only the products' own printed words.

INTEGRATION

The products are physically in this scene: same light direction, softness and colour temperature, their chrome reflects this environment, real contact shadow, real reflection on the surface below, same sharpness and grain as the scene, no outline, no halo.

KEY COMPOSITION

COBALT TUBE AND VIOLET CAPSULE ON WET BLACK GLASS, CENTRED, DARK EMPTY SPACE ABOVE, STEEL-BLUE LAB GLASS BEHIND.
