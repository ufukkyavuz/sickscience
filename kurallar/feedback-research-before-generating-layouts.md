User, 2026-11-10: B/A thumbnails in a review card were tiny and cropped to landscape - "sence anlaşılabilir mi?… Pinterest'te araştır… üretmeden önce."

**Findings (Pinterest, skincare testimonial ads):** B/A is the hero: two portrait panels side by side, edge to edge, ~55-60% of the canvas; BEFORE/AFTER tags small in corners; review text is a short pull-quote with stars below. Never put B/A as small thumbs inside a card.

**NB100/award visuals:** user does not want black backgrounds (looks like Black Friday) and wants sparse confetti: bright pale-lilac seamless, ~20-35 small pieces. See [[feedback-concept-visuals-pinterest-reference]].
