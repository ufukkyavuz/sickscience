On 2026-10-30 the user said ShapeShift looked pasted onto the pale metro seat. I answered with a new warm blue-fabric carriage. Their reply: "niye tarzı değiştirdin". The IG 04 post was then dropped.

**Why:** a complaint about one element is not a brief for a new scene; the rest of the frame was already accepted.

**How to apply:** keep the same prompt, palette and setting, change only the part they named, and say so if a new scene seems needed.
