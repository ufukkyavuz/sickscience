User (2026-10-07, SickScience): "yine hep yazıyı yukarı yazıp geçmişsin, biraz yaratıcılık kullan" — after a batch where all 4 concepts used the same centred headline + sub-line on top.

**Why:** Identical layout across a batch reads as a template and makes the feed monotonous; competitors (Sungboon, Truly, Medicube) vary structure per message.
**How to apply:** Pick the layout from the concept: left-stacked huge type with product pushed right (crop/shift the photo), numbered steps for offers/trials, parody objects (nutrition-facts label, receipt) for playful claims, "$X vs $Y" price split flanking the product for bundles. Within one batch no two concepts share the same layout. Related: [[feedback-no-template-typography]], [[feedback-no-framed-photos-be-creative]].
