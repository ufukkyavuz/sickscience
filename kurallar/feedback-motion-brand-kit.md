**How to apply:** only the font family (Founders Grotesk, no condensed, ≥32 px) and legibility/safe band are fixed. Pick colours and effects per scene and mix freely. Use `_calisma/textfx/textfx.py` (41 effects, catalogue `_calisma/textfx/out/textfx-catalogue.mp4`) and keep adding new effects learned from the motion skills. Rules doc: `MOTION-BRAND-KIT.md`.
