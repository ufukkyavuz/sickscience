User, 2026-11-16: picked the "Clinically inspired." look (Founders Grotesk Medium, ink on light), said size is not a fixed 130 px, then recorded a 9-min Figma walkthrough with voice-over (Desktop "Ekran Kaydı 2026-11-16 11.42.11.mov", editing the Vault Story/Feed/Square frames).

**Why:** layout is judged by eye per image; fixed numbers and loud add-ons make it look cheap.
**How to apply:**
1. **Photo edges:** "burada bir ayrım var, renkler çok benziyor ama insan gözü ayırt ediyor". The photo's edge must never show against the frame fill, even when colours are close. Scale the photo up (or reposition it) until it covers, then check every edge.
2. **Headline:** Medium, sized to the free area. One line when the width allows ("Clinically inspired" at ~126–128), so the text doesn't push down onto the product. Sub-line Regular, ~45. Nudge the stack up or down until it looks right ("gözüme güzel geldiği şekilde").
3. **Logo is optional:** "her zaman orada logo olması gerekmiyor". If the photo leaves no room, drop the logo or shrink the text; if a logo is wanted, fit it smaller.
4. **Feed/Square:** take the 1080×1920 master, place it behind the text, scale and position it. If the sides leave gaps, use Figma Expand on the photo. No separate image.
5. **Colour, flexed 2026-11-16:**
   - Text is black or white depending on the background.
   - A partner's identity (Ulta) is hinted at underneath, never pushed in your face:
     - a pen-drawn underline in Ulta colour;
     - a highlighter swipe behind a word;
     - a soft Ulta-colour glow behind the text.
   - A full orange headline stays rejected: "direkt göze sokmak değil, fikri alttan yedirmek daha premium".
6. **Badges (flexed 2026-11-16):** creative badges are welcome when they are useful. A gift badge works especially well ("insanlar hediye olan şeylere çok düşüyor"); confirm the gift and its threshold before writing it.
   - The free-shipping seal goes on only when the price passes the threshold. Its design can vary: white seal, a light purple glow from below, "çok mor gözüksün istemiyorum".
   - Offer and award lines are a short sub-line, e.g. "NEWBEAUTY 100".
7. **Feature badges:** a badge can show a product feature, e.g. "NX35™" with the ™ set small. A molecule graphic can sit behind it. Make it bigger than a sticker.
8. **Copy (flexed 2026-11-16):** headlines and ad lines can be our own creative writing, with clever, simple word-play ads ("markalar böyle akıllıca oyunlar yapmıyor"). The user is sending a Twitter/X example thread. Facts stay site-true: claims, stats, ingredients, prices.
9. **Ingredients:** show the ingredients that set us apart (NX35 pineapple exosomes, plant exosomes, peptides…) as classy, simple, premium scientific visuals.
