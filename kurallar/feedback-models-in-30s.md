Cast people in SickScience ad visuals in their early-to-mid 30s (user, 2026-10-01: "modelleri de daha 30lu yaşlarda yap"), even though the buyer audience is 35–60.

**Why:** the 40s-looking models in the benefit shots read too old for the aspirational look the user wants.

**How to apply:** write "in her/his early-to-mid 30s" in every people prompt; drop "mature skin / fine lines" wording but keep "natural skin texture with pores, no plastic retouching". Shoot people WIDE (35mm, waist-up, head+product ≈25% of frame height, person in the central 55% of the width) so one 9:16 master crops cleanly to story, feed and square — user asked for this after tight shots cut the product in square. Also put the face and the product in the same horizontal band, and use the correct product textures (see project CLAUDE.md).
