User, 2026-11-10: the 1106 DropOff video worked because of the pop-up/before-after, not because it was DropOff. New UGC batches must NOT be DropOff-heavy.

**Why:** retention comes from showing proof and explaining the product; static text is dull.
**How to apply:** mix ShapeShift/NetWork/PowerCycle/DropOff, favour recognisable people (Today Show), add product chips, and animate text in different ways per video (word-by-word, blur-in, slide, spin, behind the speaker). See [[reference-ugc-viral-edit-style]].
