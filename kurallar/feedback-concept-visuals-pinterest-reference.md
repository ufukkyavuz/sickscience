User, 2026-11-10 (NB100 award, after 6 flat attempts): "ödül almışız, kutlama yapılıyor, neden dümdüz beyaz arka planda karşıdan fotoğraf koyuyorsun? Pinterest'te bakıyordun, konuyla alakalı nasıl görsel yapılmış referanslı veriyordun. Yazısız üret."

**Why:** A clean product shot says nothing about the occasion. The brief is the idea (a celebration), so the picture itself must show it.

**How to apply:** For any concept/occasion visual: (1) search Pinterest in Chrome (user is logged in; tr.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=…), pick a pin that shows the occasion, download via i.pinimg.com/originals; (2) upload it in the Higgsfield composer References panel (file input, then click its thumbnail, confirm both chips: reference + element); (3) prompt "in the exact style of image 1 (camera, light, confetti/props, floor), do NOT copy its product/brand/text" + element for the product; (4) generate WITHOUT text, put headline/seal in Figma. Still no cut pedestals, floors continue to the frame edges. Never attach a person's photo ([[feedback-no-ref-image-for-people]]). Used pin: La Mer eye cream with confetti burst + halo backlight + mirror floor.
