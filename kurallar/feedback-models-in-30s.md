Cast people in SickScience ad visuals as young, beautiful women in their late 20s (user, 2026-10-04: "kadınlar genel olarak daha genç ve güzel olsunlar"; earlier 2026-10-01 rule was early-to-mid 30s), even though the buyer audience is 35–60. People shots should look like a styled studio SET (e.g. a built 'studio bathroom': white plaster walls, sculptural white vanity, 2–3 curated props, campaign lighting) — clearly a produced photo; neither a casual home snapshot nor a plain coloured seamless backdrop (user, 2026-10-04: "stüdyo setupı gibi… plato banyosu hesabı"). Breast self-check pose = fingertips on the OUTER SIDE of the breast under the raised arm, never a hand on the centre of the chest (user, 2026-10-04: "daha stüdyo çekimi gibi olsun daha profesyonel").

**Why:** the 40s-looking models in the benefit shots read too old for the aspirational look the user wants.

**How to apply:** write "a strikingly beautiful woman in her late 20s" in every people prompt; drop "mature skin / fine lines" wording but keep "natural skin texture with pores, no plastic retouching". Framing is chosen per concept, see [[feedback-lens-and-product-scale]]. Use the correct product textures (see project CLAUDE.md). Related: [[feedback-no-framed-photos-be-creative]], [[sickscience-client]].
