User, 2026-10-16, on the PowerCycle hair B/A options: "Görselleri de çok abartma ya gerçeklikten uzaklaşmayalım çok".

**Rule:**
- Make the B/A difference noticeable but natural. In prompts write "subtle, realistic, believable improvement", not "clearly / dramatically".
- Before = mild version of the concern, never extreme.
  - Hair: slightly thinner and a bit flatter, not see-through or bald.
  - Brush: a modest number of shed hairs, not a clump.
  - Cellulite: light dimpling.
- After = the same person, a little better.

**Why:** an exaggerated result reads as fake. That hurts credibility, and Meta can treat it as a misleading claim.

**How to apply:** check every B/A against the brand's own PDP before/afters (`sickscience-product-proof`), which show small, real changes.
