User, 2026-10-21, on the batch-69/70 competitor adaptations.

**Concept:** "Referansı düzgün incelemediğin için mantıklı bir zemine oturtmadın." SpoiledChild's dark ad is a joke: a vampire admires herself in a mirror ("the older I get, the younger I look"). I made a plain "reveal your glow" girl, which loses the idea. Before adapting, write down in one line what the reference *says* and how. Keep that mechanism.

**Layout:** when the user says "do it like the reference", copy the layout and device:
- Grüns emoji grid on a flat background.
- Grüns quote ad: big quote at the top left, product big on the right, flat pale background.
- Grüns "IMPORTANT UPDATE": text-led. Make it more attention-grabbing, but keep it typographic.
- An alternative is a second row; keep the version they liked.

**Colour:** green text and buttons on a gold/glitter background looked "iğrenç". Don't put product-colour text on clashing or busy backgrounds just because it's the brand colour or the reference used it.
- Default to ink text.
- Use the product colour only as an accent word.
- On coloured or busy photos, a CTA can be a white pill with product-colour text (user liked a white pill with red text on NetWork). Where the area behind is white, like the Ulta plinth, keep the filled pill.

**Partner logo on a prop:** place it where the real print would be, centred between the bag's handle knots on the front panel, at a realistic size. Not big and off-centre.

**2026-10-23, "farkı bul" reel.** The reference was a creator selfie in the background plus a CARTOON image pair in the foreground. I made product photos with a moved gel dot instead, and the user was furious.
- The adaptation keeps the mechanism: the "1 difference" between the two cartoons is the product's RESULT. Left is a cartoon woman with a double chin, right is the same woman with a sculpted jaw; for other products, hair, glow or cellulite.
- Copy the reference's layers literally: a real selfie-style woman behind, the cartoon pair in front, and the text in the same position.
- Before building, write one line on what each layer of the reference is, and mirror it.
