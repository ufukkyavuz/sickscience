User, 2026-10-11, after the flash IG set: "olmamış… instagramla uyumlu mu… daha beyaz arkaplan ağırlıklı daha premium marka algısı… grafik tasarımsal şeyler yapmalısın… sen yine performanslardaki gibi üste yazı koyup devam etmişsin."

**Why:** The brand's own grid (@sicksciencelabs) works when it looks like this:
- Light grey or white seamless backgrounds.
- The product treated as a sculptural object.
- A concept that lives in the graphic layout itself: a tear-off "Take what you need" sheet, a pineapple-exosome still life, an "What are Exosomes?" diagram, a lab coat pocket.

Dark, moody or ad-style "big headline on a photo" posts break the grid.

**How to apply** to organic IG posts (not ads):
- The background is white or off-white by default.
- Every post has a graphic idea: a diagram, a museum placard, a measuring line, a step layout, an annotation or a tear-off. Text is part of the graphic, small and refined; there is no headline slab.
- No logo when the user says so; this happened for the October series.
- Check the Instagram grid before designing a series.

**Later feedback (2026-10-11):**
- "Dümdüz beyaz podyum ne anlatıyor": the photo itself must carry a visual metaphor. Examples that worked: a marble bust carved by the gua sha, the capsule's shadow becoming a jawline profile, fluted glass blurring half the bottle, a dress form with a measuring tape.
- "Dümdüz yazı koyma": text must be graphic design tied to the photo. Examples: a gallery label card on the plinth, leader lines with dots pointing at objects, a headline split at the glass edge with the hidden half blurred, routine steps on a measuring-tape ruler, a dashed arc from product to shadow.
- Callouts must look professional ("işaretle göstermek istiyorsan daha profesyonel yap"). Never use a filled dot plus a thick diagonal line plus loose text. Standard:
  - a 22 px hairline ring with a 5 px centre dot on the target;
  - a 1.5 px elbow leader (diagonal, then horizontal);
  - the horizontal run exactly as long as the label;
  - label = index "01" (Medium) + UPPERCASE label (Regular, 6% tracking), 32 px;
  - the label sits on the side of the run away from the diagonal;
  - everything stays inside the 64 px margins.
- Code: `callout()` in the use_figma scripts of 2026-10-11.
- **Update (user, 2026-10-11): "her yerde aynı yapma".** The callout standard above is one option, never the default for every post. Pick the marking from the image's own world:
  - registration crosshair on the shadow chin;
  - white hanging specimen tags with a hole and a string on the cracked plaster;
  - a technical dimension line across the braid (white over dark hair, with a soft shadow);
  - a ring at the touch point with a leader up to the product name;
  - printed receipt typography;
  - numbered badges with a legend for the ingredient tray;
  - a museum placard on the bust.
- Measure target coordinates on a grid of the actual image before placing anything.
