Feedback relayed by the user, 2026-10-22: before/afters work better when they are our real ones wherever we have them ("Before afterları bizim gerçekler yapsak, çoğunlukla olduğu yerde daha iyi").

**How to apply:**
- Before any B/A creative, check the real sets in "SickScience Brand Assets/Images/<Product>/Before After":
  - ShapeShift: Merve K, Nasih, Naz, Hatice (Day 0 / Day 30), tugba
  - PowerCycle: PowerCycle-beforeafter1–13 (scalp and strand macros)
  - DropOff: DropOff-beforeafter1–8, body before after 2–4, plus two B/A videos in Videos/DropOff
- Use them as-is: no retouching, and the name and day stay matched to the right person, per [[feedback-ba-must-match-person]].
- AI-simulated B/A only when no real set exists for that product or angle. Those still carry "Simulated results." per CLAUDE.md.
- DropOff body B/A is a Meta-policy risk, so warn before publishing.
