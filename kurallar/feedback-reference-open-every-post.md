User, 2026-10-31, on the OneSkin 24-post grid: "Neredeyse görsellerin hepsinde hata var… Video olması gereken yerler video değil… karusel olması gereken yerler karusel değil… arka planı üretip yazıyı yazabilirdin… dümdüz bir dikdörtgen… hissiyatı vermiyor."

**Why:** I worked from the profile-grid thumbnails only. Carousels became one frame, videos became stills, and scene objects (a paper note) became flat Figma rectangles.

**How to apply:**
- Before planning, open each reference post: count the carousel slides, read every slide, watch the video and note its beats. Record format per post.
- Keep the format: carousel stays a carousel with all its slides, video stays a video.
- Copy the concept, not the subject swap: OneSkin's product in a metro seat means our product in a real location, not a product on a lab stand.
- Physical objects in a reference (paper, note, folder) are generated as real photographs with the text-bearing surface in the scene; never a flat Figma rectangle with text on it.
- Portraits: pick well-composed, centred frames; check the crop.

Same user reply: don't ask for approval on Myth vs. Fact style copy; write it from site and brand-book knowledge ("neyin mit neyin gerçek olduğunu bilmiyo musun sen"). Founder videos: use the files already on Drive, never AI. Product motion: try Flow first, else real product videos from Drive.

All reels and video-carousel clips are 1080x1920, never 4:5 (user, 2026-10-31). Offer a cover design for each video. For a scene object with writing, generate the writing inside the image when asked (the IG 13 note).
