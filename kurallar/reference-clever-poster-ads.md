**The mechanism used in every poster:** one brand asset (logo shape, product, name) is fused with a familiar object or idea. Then one short witty headline, a small sub-line, the logo, and a flat or simple background.
- Spotify logo bars = three roads with cars
- adidas stripes = mountain slope with a climber
- Nike swoosh = hockey stick
- Colgate smile = row of light bulbs
- KitKat fingers = piano keys
- McDonald's arches = tunnel
- Vicks drop = icicle
- Mastercard card = runway
- "Schrödinger's Savings" (Cowrywise)
- Photoshop "Reality is Editable"
- Apple "Dynamic Island"

**For SickScience:** the brand assets are the capsule pill silhouette (half colour, half chrome), the "Sick" + "Science" name, the product names (PowerCycle = power/restart icon, NetWork = signal bars, DropOff, ShapeShift), exosomes as delivery couriers, pineapple exosomes, and the cancer-researcher founders. Headlines may be creative; claims stay site-true.
