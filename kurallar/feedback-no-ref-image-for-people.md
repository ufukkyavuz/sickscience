When adapting a reference ad that shows a person, never attach the reference photo itself as an image reference in Higgsfield. Nano Banana Pro reproduces the same woman: same face, hair and crop. User, 2026-11-06, about batch-107: "direkt aynısını kullanma işte kadının seninki çalmak oluyo". Without the image, though, text-only prompts drifted from the pose, gaze and camera, and looked "too AI / not professional" (same day: "duruşu kamera açısı kameraya bakışı bile benzemiyo… profesyonel fotoğraf gibi değiller").

**Why:** copying a real model's likeness from a competitor ad is stealing and a legal risk. The client wants the *idea* of the shot, not their model.

**How to apply:**
- Describe the pose logic precisely in text: gaze into the lens or away, head turn, which frame edge cuts the face, finger shape and which fingertip, % positions, top colour, light.
- Cast a clearly different person: different ethnicity or features, hair colour and cut, age band.
- Ask for "professional high-end beauty campaign, beauty dish, medium format, light professional retouching that keeps real texture". Pure "unretouched" reads amateur; pure "beautiful model" reads AI.
- Generate 2 variants and compare them side by side with the reference before placing.
