On 2026-10-20 I used the batch-47 character series (renkli_/beyaz_ portraits) for the Amazon store. The user said not to use that series at all: it was made for one specific target-audience campaign. They asked for the client's original model photos instead.

**Why:** that series belongs to one campaign brief. Putting it on brand-wide surfaces like the Amazon store, PDP or general ads skews the brand's audience.

**How to apply:**
- When a general brand surface needs a model shot, search the client's Drive first: `SickScience Brand Assets/Images/<Product>/`. It is mounted locally under Drive'ım.
- Build a contact sheet with PIL and pick from it.
- Only generate new people if no original fits.
- Watch for old "Network" spelling on labels in client photos.
