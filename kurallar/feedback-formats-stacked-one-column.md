User, 2026-11-10 (angry, testimonial frames): "dikey feed ve kare formatı alt alta sırala dedim sana defalarca kez… bir sağa koyuyorsun bir sola koyuyorsun."

**Why:** He places the frames into client files himself; scattered formats mean he has to rearrange.

**How to apply:** In every Figma section: one column per variant/concept, formats stacked Story (1920) → Feed (1350) → Square (1080) with 140 px gaps; variants side by side at 1280 px spacing. Square comes from Figma Expand (desktop: layer → More → Expand → aspect menu → Square → Submit); 4:5 can be cropped from that square.
