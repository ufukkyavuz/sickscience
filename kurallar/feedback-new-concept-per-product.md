User, 2026-11-01, batch-85: after a liked ShapeShift lab × Halloween set, "diğer ürünler için de üret" got recoloured copies (same spider, cat, jar). Reply: "Farklı şeyler denesene olm hep aynı şeyi yapıyon".

**Why:** a series reads as lazy when only the colour changes; each product has its own story.

**How to apply:** keep the light and style, but write a new idea per product tied to what it does (PowerCycle hair: werewolf, Medusa, broom; DropOff body: hourglass, dress form, mummy gauze; NetWork: coffin, IV, sphere, plague mask). Vary camera too (top-down, low, macro). Stills check: no concept repeats across products.

Related: [[feedback-vary-layouts]], [[feedback-no-repeated-photos-relevant-props]], [[feedback-scale-relative-to-scene]]

**Topic first (user, same day: "Konuyla görsellerin alakası yok… shapeshift ne onunla ilgili esprili bi görsel"):** seasonal props alone are decoration. The joke must come from what the product does: ShapeShift = jawline and neck (tightening Frankenstein's neck bolt, V-line pumpkin before/after, turtleneck funeral, tech-neck skeleton). Test each idea: remove the product name; does the picture still point at the jaw or neck? If not, rewrite it.
