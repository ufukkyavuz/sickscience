User, 2026-10-30, on the competitor offer series: "bundan sonra burda daha fazla bilimsel tarzda ilerleyebilmek için zemin ve arkaplanları beyaz ağırlıklı gitmemiz lazım bence".

**Why:** the client wants more science-looking content, and white grounds read as lab/clinical.

**How to apply:** in new SickScience visuals, make grounds and backgrounds mostly white or very light (white lab bench, frosted glass, clean glassware). Coloured or dark grounds only when a reference or concept truly needs it. This strengthens the direction in [[feedback-no-repeated-photos-relevant-props]]; it is not a ban on every other background.

Same session: read references accurately. The Cécred "25% OFF" type is water droplets on a pale white-grey ground, not chrome; I misread it. Look closely at a reference's material before building it. A review-style card must look like a real person's comment (avatar, name), not a brand statement.
