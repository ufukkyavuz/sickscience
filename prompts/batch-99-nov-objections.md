## 3. Site answers we can use, verbatim

- **Time to results.**
  - ShapeShift: "judge your results at about 4 weeks"; "A visibly sharper jawline in about 4 weeks".
  - PowerCycle: "Fuller, thicker-looking hair in 4 weeks", "50 participants, results were measured at 28 and 56 days".
  - DropOff: "Visible change in 2 weeks".
- **Risk.**
  - "we accept returns within 30 days of purchase".
  - "Can I return a product if I have already opened it? Yes, as long as you have used less than 1/4 of the product".
  - DropOff: "Try DropOff free for 21 days. Pay only shipping today and decide after".
- **Shipping.** "Standard shipping is FREE … for those who subscribe to email, and for everyone on orders over $75."
- **Skin.**
  - "All people, all skin types can use SickScience products."
  - "Hypoallergenic and Dermatologist-Tested and Fragrance-Free".
  - ShapeShift: "vegan and free of fragrance and dyes".
- **Usage.**
  - ShapeShift: "jaw, under-chin and neck, morning and night, before moisturizer. Smooth it on with upward strokes".
  - PowerCycle: "section by section … circular motions … No rinsing; style as usual."
- **How long a bottle lasts.** The FAQ answer "We recommend using our products within 12 months after opening" is shelf life, NOT how long a bottle lasts. Do not use it for a cost-per-day line.
- **Not to use.**
  - DropOff "reduction in circumference" (fat claim, policy risk).
  - The "safe for pregnant women" answer (medical, not an ad claim).
  - "clinically proven" for anything except PowerCycle, which is the only product where the site says it.

## 4. Concepts, one objection per ad

Funnel stage, then the viewer's objection, then the answer and the format. Each concept gets its own look; real client photos and UGC come first (see CLAUDE.md "Müşteri stüdyo çekimleri önce").

### TOFU (problem aware, does not know us)
1. **"What even is an exosome?"** Format: lab scene, one big object (pineapple beaker S_0880 style). Copy from the site's NX35 line: "plant-based biomimetic exosome technology, designed to support cell-to-cell communication." Headline draft: *Skincare that talks to your cells.* No offer in TOFU; one small "Early Black Friday" chip only if it is the EBF window.
2. **"Who made this?"** Format: founders on camera (Merve challenge intro, cloned voice reads). "Made by two PhD scientists" is already in the banner copy. Headline: *Two PhD scientists. One jawline serum.*
3. **"Is it for my skin?"** Format: a grid of 4 real faces or hands from UGC with one line each. Answer: "All people, all skin types…" + "Hypoallergenic, Dermatologist-Tested, Fragrance-Free". Headline: *Sensitive skin? Read the label.*

### MOFU (considering, has objections)
4. **"How long until I see anything?" (ShapeShift)** Format: calendar or countdown graphic, week 1 → week 4, the real Merve diary frames on the days. Answer: "judge your results at about 4 weeks". Headline: *Give it 4 weeks. Then judge.*
5. **"How long until I see anything?" (PowerCycle)** Format: data card, the study as the visual (50 people, day 28, day 56, +29% thickness). Headline: *50 people. 56 days. Measured.*
6. **"Will I actually use it every day?"** Format: 15-second routine reel, real GRWM footage (Merve), the two steps on screen. Answer: the PDP usage text. Headline: *One to two pumps. Upward. Done.* (PDP dose: "1–2 pumps".)
7. **"Do the results hold up?"** Format: real before/after from Brand Assets (client real B/A first), one held split. Stats verbatim (92% / 94% / 98% / 90%). Headline: *92% saw a more sculpted jawline.*
8. **"Which one is for me?"** Format: quiz-style carousel, 4 concerns → 4 products (jaw / glow / hair / body). Ends on the Vault as "all four concerns". Headline: *Pick your problem.*

### BOFU (ready, needs a push or a safety net)
9. **"Is it worth the price?"** Format: the sum on screen. Bundle price vs. the singles added up (Vault: SS $64 + PC $64 + DO $52 = $180 → EBF price). Headline: *Three serums. One price.*
10. **"What if it doesn't work for me?"** Format: plain text-message or FAQ-card UI. Answer verbatim: "returns within 30 days… even opened, as long as you have used less than 1/4". Headline: *Opened it? Still returnable.*
11. **"I'm not sure about DropOff."** Format: trial offer card. "Try DropOff free for 21 days. Pay only shipping today." Headline: *21 days. Just pay shipping.* Run outside the BF dates or check that the trial still runs during the sale.
12. **"Shipping will kill the deal."** Format: cart screenshot UI, total crosses $75, shipping turns FREE. Headline: *Your set ships free.* Use only on sets whose discounted price stays over $75 (check the table: only Vault and GLP-1 stay over in every window, Award Duo drops to $64.80 at Cyber Monday; Body Duo drops to $73.50 in EBF if the discount comes off the sale price; Sculpted and the On-the-Go sets are under $75).
13. **"I'll wait for a better deal."** Format: countdown from frame 0 (rule: big, from 00:59). Shows the ladder: EBF bundles 25% → BF 30% → CM 40%, each with its dates. Headline per window: *25% off sets ends Nov 21.* / *30% off everything ends Nov 28.* / *40% off. 48 hours.*

### Ulta track (separate creatives, Ulta rules: @ultabeauty, #UltaBeauty, official logo)
14. **"Can I get it near me?"** (BF/CM) Format: real Ulta store visit footage (Dropbox "Ulta UGC visit content"). Headline: *30% off at Ulta. In store and online.*
15. **"App only?"** (15–21 Nov) Format: phone UI, the Ulta app with the deal tag. Headline: *Early Black Friday, in the Ulta app.*
