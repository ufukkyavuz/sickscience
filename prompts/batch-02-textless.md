Ortak kapanış (her prompt'un sonuna): COPY_SPACE + REALISM

COPY_SPACE = "Vertical 9:16 composition. Keep the top 14% of the frame calm and empty. Leave a large clean, uncluttered area of plain background between 15% and 42% of the frame height for headline text to be added later. Place the products in the lower-middle of the frame, between roughly 45% and 68% of the height. The bottom third continues the same calm background/surface with no important detail. Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no buttons, no graphics added to the scene — the only lettering allowed is the products' own printed labels."

REALISM = "Ultra photorealistic, hyperrealistic, indistinguishable from a real photograph, 8K detail. All product text must be perfectly legible, sharp, and accurate — no blurry or distorted letters."
