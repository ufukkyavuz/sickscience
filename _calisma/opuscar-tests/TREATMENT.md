# ShapeShift: "The Programme" (Swiss Motion Graphics test)

## Brief
- **One sentence:** ShapeShift's results are measured: four numbers, one serum, a jawline in 4 weeks.
- **Audience:** women aged 35–60 on Meta (Reels and Stories).
- **Duration and format:** 18 s; 1080×1920 master at 24 fps. Feed 4:5 and Square crops come from the same master (SickScience rule).
- **Real copy only.** Site PDP "Real Results", verbatim:
  - 92% saw a more sculpted jawline
  - 94% saw smoother tech-neck lines
  - 98% saw firmer, tighter neck skin
  - 90% noticed improved skin definition
- **Headline:** "Chisel Your Jawline in 4 Weeks". The client changed 2 → 4 weeks on 2026-10-21.
- **Product line:** "ShapeShift · V-Line Jaw Defining Serum · $64".
- **Assets:**
  - Founders Grotesk (brand font; no condensed cuts).
  - SickScience logo (black and pink) exported from Figma component 29:219.
  - Product drawn as a flat graphic in Swiss print language: a capsule with a purple top half, silver-grey bottom half and a black stripe carrying white vertical type. No photo is pasted.
- **Avoid:**
  - centred big text on a gradient, fades, a logo pop at the end
  - any number or claim not on the PDP
  - a footnote
  - text under 32 px

## Three structures
1. **Countdown:** 98 → 94 → 92 → 90 as a falling ladder. It reads as decline, which is wrong for a results ad.
2. **Comparison spread:** before on the left, after on the right. The comparison would have to be invented, because we have no measured "before" numbers.
3. **Programme (chosen):** the four results build a strict grid one by one. The bars are the system. The product is the one exception: it enters linear, unsnapped, off-grid, and the system re-flows around it. A Swiss native move fits the brand story directly: everyone else's grid, our product breaks it.

## Logline and arc
The page fills with proof, one hard number per beat, until the product cuts through the system and takes its place.
- **Setup:** a giant 92 fills the frame and turns out to be one module.
- **Turn:** the purple exception arrives.
- **End:** the headline sits flush left beside the product; logo.

## Benchmark
- **Learn from 1960s Zurich posters:** scale contrast, flush left, one signal colour.
- **Learn from identity systems in motion:** every snap lands on a beat, and deletions are knife cuts.
- **Don't take:** any specific poster layout, Helvetica, other brands' colours.

## Beat sheet and cue map
- **Tempo:** 120 bpm (0.5 s beats), 36 beats.
- **Sections:**
  - A: kick and closed hat motorik, from 0 s.
  - B: marimba ostinato added at bar 2 (2 s).
  - C: drums drop, only a sine glide that follows the capsule (9.5–12 s).
  - D: full band back on the downbeat at 12 s.
  - End: a hard stop at 17.5 s, ring-out.
- **Hits:**
  - 0.0: numeral thunk
  - 0.5, 1.0, 1.5: numeral climbs, one block snap each
  - 2.0: "%" stamp
  - 2.5: ruling-pen "tss" as bar 1 draws
  - 4.5, 5.5, 6.5: snaps for bars 2–4 (letterpress clack)
  - 8.5: near-silence
  - 9.5: exception enters (sine glide, no drums)
  - 12.0: guillotine snicks, 4 knife cuts on 16ths
  - 12.5: headline line 1
  - 13.0: line 2
  - 14.0: caption row
  - 15.5: logo wipe
  - 16.0: price stamp
  - 17.5: hard stop

## Type
- One family: Founders Grotesk Regular, Medium, Semibold and Bold.
- Flush left on a 6-column grid: 72 px margins, 24 px gutters.
- Sizes:
  - giant numerals 560 px
  - statements 64 px
  - captions 34 px (minimum 32)
- No burned subtitles (no voice).
- Meta safe band: all copy stays between y 270 and y 1250 in the final state. The bottom 35 % stays calm paper.
