This replaces the 2026-10-01 wide-framing rule (35mm, waist-up, head and product about 25% of frame) and the 2026-10-04 ban on enlarging the product by moving it toward the lens.

**Why:** framing wide for crop safety kept products small and scenes flat. Figma Expand solves the format problem, so framing can serve the idea.

**How to apply:** pick lens and crop per concept, for example ultra-wide close-up, macro, or overhead. Close foreground product is fine. Still hold product proportions, half cap, white label and PowerCycle bigger than ShapeShift/NetWork in the same plane (see [[sickscience-product-dimensions]]). Same image across formats still applies, extended with [[feedback-expand-in-figma]]. Prompt sections: [[feedback-photographic-prompt-format]].

Campaign variety: references and audiences differ, so do not tie a campaign to one visual world. Background, model, light and layout may change per creative. See [[feedback-vary-layouts]].
