Preserve the exact identity of every product from its element: shape, proportions, relative size, colours, construction and printed branding. BACKGROUND

Deep slate grey-blue falling to near black at the top. Wet black glass surface with fine water droplets and a mirror reflection of the products.

PHOTOGRAPHIC FINISH

f/5.6. Monochrome steel-blue grade: everything is silver, slate and blue-grey, the only saturated colours are the violet capsule and the navy tube. Fine photographic grain.
