# Brief: SickScience brand story, told by drawing (2026-11-05)

Source: the Fathom meeting "Sick Science brand book" (Nov 5, 37 min), with Merve, Polen, Zeynep and Ufuk. Action item @21:27: "Produce explainer video: plant exosomes = drones/trucks".

## The message in one sentence
Two geneticists spent 14 years in the lab with exosomes; a deeply personal reason pulled them out of it, and they put that technology into formulas you can use at home.

## Story beats (the founders' own words, from the meeting)
1. **2009, a first chemistry/biology class:** Merve and Polen meet. They have worked together for 14 years. Both are geneticists with master's and PhD degrees in biotechnology.
2. **The lab:** Merve did cancer research (Harvard). Polen did regenerative medicine and wound healing (MD Anderson). They hold international patents and 20–30 peer-reviewed papers.
3. **The personal reason:** Merve's mother had mesothelioma, a cancer with no cure. The research was still preclinical. After she passed away, they finished the research and published it.
4. **What an exosome is (the drawing analogy):** tiny "cargo" bubbles that cells release, carrying the cell's message. Drawn as drones or trucks.
5. **Plants:** pineapple, garlic, wheat, radish. They built a library of thousands of plant "messages".
6. **NX35:** a way to separate exosomes, plus stabilization (no cold chain, stable at room temperature), plus the library. All three together are NX35.
7. **Why beauty:** "Everyone says they're a scientist, but they're marketers." The goal was home formulas as effective as in-clinic procedures, scientific but never boring.
8. **The name:** when the La Mer marketing director saw the data: "Oh my god, that's sick!" "No, that's science." → SickScience.
9. **Close:** the four products plus the logo. "Science, but never boring."

## Claim check (US, Meta, FTC)
- ❌ **Not used:** "a woman with the same cancer took our exosomes and her tumors shrank, she survived". This is a medical cure claim, and the ad would be read as "exosomes cure cancer". Telling it is fine; using it as a healing promise is not. It stays the founders' own story, told in interviews.
- ✅ **Safe:** "My mom had an incurable cancer. We were still in the lab. After she was gone, we finished the research" → "and decided to take it out of the lab." The motivation is told; no promise of results.
- ❌ **Avoid:** "fat burning", "cure", "permanent". The DropOff clinical result as cm is not used (fat claim risk).
- ✅ Patents, papers, and the Harvard / MD Anderson names: the founders said these can be used. Before use, we confirm the exact wording ("worked with / at") with the founders.

## Decisions (user, 2026-11-05)
- The cancer story is told only as motivation. The patient recovery is never told.
- VO alternates between Merve and Polen.
