User, 2026-11-01, batch-85e (tech-neck skeleton, capsule far right): a product pushed to the side leaves the safe zone, stops being seen, and in Reels the Instagram UI can land on it, so the message doesn't get across.

**Why:** the Reels right-hand icon column covers roughly the right 15% of the frame. The old rule only protected top and bottom bands and 6% side margins, so a product could pass the check and still be hidden.

**How to apply:**
- Keep the whole product, and whatever carries the idea (neck, fangs, jawline), inside 15–85% of the frame width; prefer the central 60%.
- Write that band as explicit percentages in every COMPOSITION section, then measure the output bbox before delivery. Same habit as the vertical band in [[reference-nano-banana-placement]].
- Never fix it by moving text to a side column ([[feedback-text-centered-like-reference]]); change the photo or crop.
