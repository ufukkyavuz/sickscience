# Style guide v2: the realistic lab notebook (2026-11-05)
This replaces the cream-paper illustration version (style-guide.md, S6/S9). The reference mechanisms are in REFERENCE-ANALYSIS.md.

## World
- **The object:** a real hardcover lab notebook with a grid page (5 mm grid in light blue-grey). The header has the fields "PROJECT ____ DATE ____ PAGE 01" printed on it. The notebook lies open on a white lab bench.
- **Around it (on the edges, never on the page):** a pipette, one petri dish, a small drop of gel-serum, and a fine-liner pen cap. White-dominant, scientific (2026-10-30 rule).
- **Light:** soft daylight from upper left, the page edge casting a shadow, paper curl near the spine. 50–85 mm look, f/4–5.6: the page is sharp and the edges fall off slightly.
- **Imperfections:** paper fibre, a light page wave near the spine, faint ink show-through from the previous page, grain, a few specks of dust (IMPERFECTIONS rule).

## Writing
- **Ink:** black 0.4 fine-liner, written with code stroke by stroke. Pressure variation, a darker dot at each line's start, a slightly glossy wet line for 0.3 s that then turns matte.
- **Handwriting:** one real handwriting font family (handwriting is a prop, the exception in CLAUDE.md). Small notes are about 1/3 the size of the BIG words. The baseline wanders slightly with the grid, and the letters aren't perfect.
- **Highlighter:** a real highlighter stroke with uneven edges, slightly darker at both ends, multiply blend. Yellow is for dates and numbers, violet (#A921C0 family) for SickScience, and product colours only for the four product names at the end.
- **Crossing out:** one or two quick pen strikes. A cross-out is the story's turn: "lab → at home" and "scientist? → marketer."
- **Quiet beat (mom):** small handwriting only, no highlighter, no SFX, the music thins out.

## Photos on the page
- Printed photos (white border, slightly matte, with a little glare) are fixed with washi tape. Each is captioned by hand: "FIG. 01 · PINEAPPLE" and so on.
- The photo content is real or generated petri-dish/plant photos. No product inside them.

## Hand
- Option A: a real hand with a pen as a generated video plate, with the pen tip tracked to the code's stroke tip. It's risky: fingers can deform. Check the anatomy.
- **Option B (recommended):** the hand stays out of frame, and only the pen tip and its soft shadow move along the stroke (the pen is a real-photo PNG; it is lit and shadowed in the scene, not pasted). This avoids the floating 3D pen in INO and looks more realistic.

## Camera
- The film opens on the finished page (0–1 s), with a quick whip back up to the page header, and the camera then follows the pen downward. There's no digital zoom: slow push-ins and a light handheld drift (the amplitude stays small).
- **End card:** the camera lifts up, and the four real products stand on the bench beside the notebook. They are photographed in the same scene (a Nano Banana composite with the elements); a PNG is never pasted.

## Sound
- A real fine-liner scratch (varying per word), the rip of washi tape, a page rustle, and a soft piano/plucked bed. Merve and Polen alternate on VO. -14 LUFS.

## Formats
- 9:16 master. Text and critical notes stay in the 270–1250 px band. The page is wider than the frame, so the camera pans.
- Organic about 50 s. Ad cut ≤20 s.
