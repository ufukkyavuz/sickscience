User, 2026-10-21, on the six offer films. Applies to all future videos:

> "genel olarak daha temiz beyaz arkaplanlı, bilimsel şekilde görülüp düşünülebilecek tarzlarda gidelim, çok abartmadan… sonraki videolar için geçerli olsun"

**Look:** clean, white or very light background, a scientific and credible feel, restrained. No dark or glitzy stages by default, no over-designed gimmicks.

**Sound:** "gereksiz çok fazla ses efekti kullanıyosun".
- Keep a quiet tick, one strike and one ding; the music breathes.
- No exaggerated entrances; the swinging tag felt "abartılı".

**Bottom zone:** the bottom 35 % is not empty space.
- Only critical copy (countdown, price, save) stays in y 270–1250.
- Products and our SHOP NOW button can sit lower and bigger. Meta's CTA covers that area anyway.

**Layout:** never squeeze everything into one area. Give each element room.

**No framed boxes:** a board or panel motif goes full-bleed, never inside a framed box. This is the same rule as [[feedback-no-framed-photos-be-creative]].

The user liked the first marquee version's top and bottom ticker bands.

Related: [[feedback-countdown-first]], [[feedback-code-films-verdict]], [[feedback-ig-organic-premium]]

**Round 4 additions (2026-10-21).**

**Nested shapes:** use concentric radii. Inner radius = outer radius − inset. A pill inside a pill is a pill, never a square-ish badge in a round pill.

**SHOP NOW placement:**
- Not at the very bottom of the frame.
- Put it higher, inside the copy block, if there's a natural spot.
- In crowded films, drop it; Meta has its own button.
- Sparse films may keep it.

**Elements dropping in from the top** (receipt, banner): leave clear space under them so they never look cramped.

**Music (2026-10-21):** "Ses aşırı rahatsız edici" about the raw numpy sine pads, ticks and bell chimes.
- Never ship hand-synthesized sine beds or bells.
- Use the sampled mgaudio recipes (`~/motion-tools/mg-styles-15/lib/audio`). For clean or texture films, `recipes.ambient(flavor='piano')`, exported at about −16 LUFS, with no SFX.
- Backups of the replaced versions are in ~/sickscience-work/b74/remix/old.
