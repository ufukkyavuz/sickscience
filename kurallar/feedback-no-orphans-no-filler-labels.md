User 2026-10-20 (Oprah Daily PR creative): "tepedeki beauty yazısı ne alaka… bazı yazılar kesik kalmış".

**Why:** The user read a quote ending in "…" and single words wrapped onto their own line ("Foundation.", "TO BCRF", "Serum") as cut-off text. A category kicker copied from the site's UI added meaning-free clutter.

**How to apply:**
- Quote a full sentence ending in a period. Use "…" only in the middle of a quote, never at the end.
- Give manual, balanced line breaks (\n) for every format width. No line may hold a single orphan word. Check each format's screenshot.
- Don't add decorative labels (category kickers, eyebrows) unless they carry information the viewer needs.
