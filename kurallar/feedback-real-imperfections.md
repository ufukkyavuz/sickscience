**Why:** spotless surfaces, perfect chrome and noise-free backgrounds make the generated images read as CGI/AI. Real product photos always carry small flaws.

**How to apply:** every photographic prompt gets an IMPERFECTIONS paragraph after PHOTOGRAPHIC FINISH, written per scene:
- a few specks of dust on dark or glossy surfaces
- faint hairline scratches and micro swirl marks on the floor/plinth, never on the product labels
- a light fingerprint smudge or water spot on glass
- slight unevenness in the backdrop paper or plaster
- real film grain and sensor noise in the shadows
- a touch of lens bloom on the hottest highlights, mild vignetting

Keep it subtle: product labels stay clean and legible, and the product itself gets at most a soft dust speck or two. Remove "spotless, flawless, perfectly clean" wording. Negatives get "CGI-clean surfaces, perfectly smooth gradients, plastic perfection".
