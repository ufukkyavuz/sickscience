A real dramatic product photograph. The idea: one ShapeShift capsule alone in a single hard shaft of white light in total darkness, like an object revealed on one night only.

Image 1 is a real photo of the ShapeShift capsule and shows the true construction of every capsule in this brand: a slim rounded pill, its glossy coloured chrome cap is exactly the upper half, the lower half is mirror-polished silver chrome, rounded at both ends. The brand words are printed in thin pure white letters running vertically, directly on the chrome of the cap and of the silver half. The word SickScience is printed only on the coloured cap, and the product name (ShapeShift, NetWork or PowerCycle) only on the silver half, each once. There is NO printed band, strip or label behind the letters. PROPORTIONS ARE CRITICAL: the ShapeShift and NetWork capsules are slim and tall, height 3.4 times width, never stubby. Image 2 is a second real photo of the same ShapeShift capsule. Exactly one product, no extra product.

PRODUCT ORIENTATION

The capsule stands upright, label facing the camera, text vertical. Camera low, level with the middle of the capsule.

COMPOSITION AND FRAMING

Vertical 9:16 frame, 100mm lens. The capsule is horizontally centred, from 54% to 80% of the frame height. A hard vertical shaft of white light falls from the top of the frame onto it, visible as a faint column in very light haze, and makes a sharp bright ellipse on the black floor around it. The whole band from 8% to 48% of the frame height is dark, only crossed by the faint light shaft, empty for a very large headline.

SCALE

The capsule is 12 cm tall and 3.5 cm wide. The light ellipse on the floor is about three times the capsule width.

LIGHT AND SHADOW

Single hard top light. Bright crisp highlight down the violet cap and the silver half, deep falloff to black at the sides. A faint frontal fill so the white printed words read clearly.

BACKGROUND

Total black. Glossy black floor with a soft mirror reflection of the capsule inside the light ellipse.

PHOTOGRAPHIC FINISH

f/8. Mirror chrome reflecting the light ellipse, very faint atmospheric haze only inside the light shaft. Neutral grade, inky blacks, the only colour is violet. Fine photographic grain. Ultra photorealistic, hyperrealistic, indistinguishable from a real photograph, 8K detail. All product text must be perfectly legible, sharp, and accurate, no blurry or distorted letters. No text, no letters, no logos added to the scene; only the products' own printed words.

INTEGRATION

The products are physically in this scene: same light direction, softness and colour temperature, their chrome reflects this environment, real contact shadow, real reflection on the surface below, same sharpness and grain as the scene, no outline, no halo.

KEY COMPOSITION

ONE VIOLET CAPSULE IN A HARD SHAFT OF WHITE LIGHT, LOW IN THE FRAME, TOTAL BLACK, HUGE EMPTY SPACE ABOVE.
