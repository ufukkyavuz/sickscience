# PowerCycle: "Contour → Open → Proof" (3D launch-film test)

## Brief
- **One sentence:** the award-winning scalp serum, shown as a real object: open it, see the formula, read the numbers.
- **Audience:** men and women 35–60 with thinning-looking hair (Meta Reels and Stories).
- **Format:** 15.0 s; 1080×1920 at 24 fps. Feed 4:5 and Square are crops of this master.
- **Product:** the real PowerCycle, modelled in three.js from the client's packshots (ERH_7460B front, 7462B open, 7464B cap).
  - Proportions are measured from the PNG: h/w 3.27; cap = upper 46 %; domed top; flat base with about 0.24·w fillets.
  - Narrow black stripe (0.145·w) with white Founders Grotesk Semibold text, reading bottom → top.
  - Open state: white ribbed collar and bulb, silver neck ring, glass pipette.
- **Copy, verbatim only:**
  - "Esquire Grooming Awards 2026 Winner"
  - "PowerCycle" / "Hair Growth Scalp Serum"
  - "On average" +29% strand diameter · +20% volume · +16% length
  - "Thicker hair in 60 days" · "$64"
- **Brand:** black, white, logo pink #C027B3, PowerCycle green. Logo in white "Sick" + pink "Science" on dark. Founders Grotesk only, no condensed cuts, no text under 32 px.
- **Layout:** all type inside y 270–1250; the bottom 35 % is floor and reflection only.

## Three structures
1. **Line-up of states:** closed → open → dropper out, three locked plates. Clear, but static; no peak.
2. **Inside first:** start inside the bottle on the gel and pull out through the glass. The bottle is opaque chrome, so this would mean inventing a transparent product. Rejected (real product only).
3. **Contour → Open → Proof (chosen):** a sweep finds the chrome in the dark, the award names it, the cap pops on the downbeat (signature move), the dropper rises and one gel drop falls, the numbers land on beats, and the cap snaps back for the end card. One object, one turn (sealed → open → proven).

## Benchmark
- **Learn from premium dark-field launch films:** strip-light sweeps drawing the contour; slow orbits; cuts only on the beat; one accent colour.
- **Don't take:** any silhouette, typeface or sound of those films.

## Cue map
- **Tempo:** 120 BPM (0.5 s beats), 30 beats.
- **Sections:**
  - 0–4: sub drone and glass shimmer on the sweep; a single glass bell on 0.25 (hook).
  - 4–5.75: soft kick and tick grid; a noise riser 5.0–5.75.
  - 5.75–6.0: silence.
  - 6.0: cap pop (magnetic click cluster plus a glass chord ×2.76/×5.4) and an 808 hit.
  - 6.25–7.0: an air puff as the dropper lifts.
  - 7.5: drop "plip" plus a high glass ding.
  - 7.75–11.5: groove (kick, clap, 808 line, FM glass plucks); one pluck per stat at 7.75 / 8.0 / 8.25.
  - 11.5: a reverse swell.
  - 12.0: cap snap (click cluster) and the final chord; ring-out to 15.
