A real high-end campaign photograph of the whole range. The idea: four formulas lined up on a long black mirror bench under one cold beam, a vast dark space above them.

Image 1 is a real photo of the ShapeShift capsule and shows the true construction of every capsule in this brand: a slim rounded pill, its glossy coloured chrome cap is exactly the upper half, the lower half is mirror-polished silver chrome, rounded at both ends. The brand words are printed in thin pure white letters running vertically, directly on the chrome of the cap and of the silver half. The word SickScience is printed only on the coloured cap, and the product name (ShapeShift, NetWork or PowerCycle) only on the silver half, each once. There is NO printed band, strip or label behind the letters. PROPORTIONS ARE CRITICAL: the ShapeShift and NetWork capsules are slim and tall, height 3.4 times width, never stubby. Image 2 shows the NetWork capsule: glossy RED chrome cap, label 'NetWork' with a capital N and capital W. Image 3 shows the PowerCycle capsule: glossy GREEN chrome cap, larger. Image 4 shows the DropOff tube: matte cobalt blue squeeze tube on its blue cap. Take every capsule's construction from image 1: no band behind the letters, white letters directly on the chrome. Exactly four products: violet ShapeShift capsule, red NetWork capsule, green PowerCycle capsule, cobalt DropOff tube. No extra product.

PRODUCT ORIENTATION

All four stand upright in one slightly staggered row, from left to right: green PowerCycle capsule, violet ShapeShift capsule, cobalt DropOff tube slightly behind, red NetWork capsule. Labels face the camera, text vertical. Camera low, level with the capsule middles.

COMPOSITION AND FRAMING

Vertical 9:16 frame, 85mm lens. The row is horizontally centred inside the middle 70% of the frame width and stands from 56% to 80% of the frame height, products small and elegant. The black mirror bench fills the bottom 20% with long reflections. The whole band from 8% to 50% of the frame height is empty deep dark space for a very large headline.

SCALE

If the ShapeShift capsule is 100 tall, NetWork is 100, PowerCycle is 117 tall and 25% wider, the DropOff tube is 165. Real sizes: ShapeShift and NetWork 12 cm tall, 3.5 cm wide; PowerCycle 14 cm tall, 4.5 cm wide; DropOff 20 cm tall.

LIGHT AND SHADOW

One narrow cold white beam from straight above lights the row and fades upward into darkness. Crisp vertical highlights on every chrome cap and on the tube. Soft frontal fill keeps all printed words readable.

BACKGROUND

Pure deep charcoal-black, with a faint vertical glow of the beam behind the products. Nothing else.

PHOTOGRAPHIC FINISH

f/8. Mirror chrome, matte tube plastic, glossy black bench reflections. Neutral silver grade, deep blacks, the only saturated colours are the four product colours. Fine photographic grain. Ultra photorealistic, hyperrealistic, indistinguishable from a real photograph, 8K detail. All product text must be perfectly legible, sharp, and accurate, no blurry or distorted letters. No text, no letters, no logos added to the scene; only the products' own printed words.

INTEGRATION

The products are physically in this scene: same light direction, softness and colour temperature, their chrome reflects this environment, real contact shadow, real reflection on the surface below, same sharpness and grain as the scene, no outline, no halo.

KEY COMPOSITION

FOUR PRODUCTS IN ONE ROW LOW IN THE FRAME ON A BLACK MIRROR BENCH, ONE COLD BEAM FROM ABOVE, HUGE EMPTY DARK SPACE ABOVE.
