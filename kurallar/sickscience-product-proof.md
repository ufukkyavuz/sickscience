Researched from the PDPs ("Real Results" blocks and the brand's own B/A images) on 2026-10-16, after the user said "ürünleri yanlış yorumlamışsın… powercycle senin yaptığın gibi değil".

- **PowerCycle:** "Stronger, Fuller-Looking Hair in 2 Weeks".
  - Stats: 100% improvement of hair thickness · 29% average increase in hair thickness (hair diameter) · 89% improvement of hair density · 20% more hair volume.
  - Also listed: "Reduces Shedding".
  - The site B/A is a **trichoscope macro of the strands**: thicker strands, more per follicle.
  - It is NOT a receded-hairline or bald-patch regrowth. Show strand thickness, density, volume, part/ponytail fullness, or less shedding.
- **ShapeShift:** "Chisel Your Jawline in 2 Weeks".
  - Stats: 92% / 94% / 98% / 90%.
  - The site B/A shows a sagging jaw and neck from a three-quarter angle, then tighter.
- **NetWork:** "Reveal Your Glow in 2 Weeks".
  - Stats: 94% smoother-feeling skin · 90% visibly brighter complexion · 100% healthier-looking glow · 94% moisturized.
  - Targets uneven tone, texture and wrinkles, plus discoloration and pores.
  - The site B/A is smile lines and texture. Glow and texture lead; dark spots are secondary.
- **DropOff:** "A More Sculpted Body. Visible Change in 2 Weeks".
  - Stats: 92% thighs & belly circumference · 88% reduced cellulite & dimpling · 87% smoother texture · 98% nourished.
  - The site B/A is buttocks and thighs with cellulite, labelled Day 0 / Day 56.
- The site B/A images are only 754×414. Ask the client for originals before using them in ads.
- The site marks the treated area with a dotted circle. The v2 countdown layout reuses that idea (`_calisma/batch-48-countdown/render48v2.py`).

**How to apply:** before any B/A or result visual, read this note and depict the PDP mechanism. Use PDP wording for headline and stat.
