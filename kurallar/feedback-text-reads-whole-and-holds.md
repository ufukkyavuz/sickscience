User, angry, 2026-10-30, on the Polen reel: "kadın cümlesini bitirmeden yazılar geçiyor gidiyor… yarısı gözüküyor, yarısı gözükmüyor."

**Why:** words were timed to shots rather than speech, and big text behind the subject lost letters to her head, hand and bag.

**How to apply:**
- Time every text from VO word timings; hold it to sentence end + about 0.25 s, across cuts if needed.
- Text behind a subject: measure the subject's top per frame from the masks and place lines above it, so depth never costs a letter. If the frame has no free wall (hallway full-body), put the text in front.
- Animate whole letters (fade + small rise). No clip-line reveals.
- End text before a hand or prop reaches it.
- Check zoomed frames at the moments props move, not just a contact sheet.
