User, 2026-10-21, on the six offer films: "geri sayımlar direkt gelmeli en son değil, buranın en iyi geri dönüş yapacak şeyi o zaten ve 00:59'dan geri saymalı".

**Why:** the countdown is the best-performing element of these ads. Hiding it until the end wastes the hook.

**How to apply:** for any countdown, discount or offer video or static:
- The timer is large in the top half from the first frame.
- It stays on screen the whole time, ticking audibly.
- It starts at 00:59 in MM:SS format.
- Price, strike, pill and CTA follow after it.
