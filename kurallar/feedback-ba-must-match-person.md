User (2026-10-07, SickScience TODAY videos): I showed a young woman's before/after while the host said "Kathy, 65" and headlined "SHE'S 65" — user: "65 yaşında diye gösterilen kadın 65 yaşında değil". Also: "her seferinde aynı before afterları göstermek yerine… farklı farklı… kullan".

**Why:** A mismatched age/name on a B/A is a false claim (and a Meta policy risk); repeating one B/A makes all ads look the same.
**How to apply:** Only attach a name/age/duration to a B/A when the source clearly links them (Kathy 65 = grey-haired neck close-up in `Products/today-show/shapeshift/TodaysShow-1080_1080.mp4` 18.8–23.6 s; clean panels in `~/OpenMontage/projects/today-perf/assets/ba_*_before/after.png`). Otherwise keep copy generic. Rotate different B/A sets per video/card (sources: TodaysShow-1080_1080, 6.mp4, 1080x1920.mp4, site PDP images). Related: [[feedback-no-framed-photos-be-creative]].
